Brand guidelines

The system behind Scaleterra: logo, color, type, and voice. One reference so everything we ship looks and sounds like the same company. v1, evolving.

01 · Logo

The mark

Terraced strata rising to a lit peak: scale (rising) plus terra (earth, layers). Clay at the base to ochre at the top. Reads on dark and light. Note: this is a working mark. It's a generic shape (low legal risk, but not ownable), so it stays a placeholder until we commission a distinctive mark before public launch.

04 · Voice

Grounded, specific, no theater

We sell outcomes to hands-on operators who are often wary of tech and AI. Talk like a sharp operator, not a SaaS brochure.

GroundedConfidentWarm-industrialSpecific over aphoristic
Write this

"We found Messina $206,000. Same revenue, same doors open."

Not this

"Our AI-powered platform empowers you to unlock seamless, next-gen margin optimization."

Write this

"No new system to learn. We read what you already run on."

Not this

"Leverage our robust, enterprise-grade, all-in-one solution."

Rules: lead with the money or the outcome. Name real numbers. No buzzwords (streamline, empower, seamless, robust, next-gen). No em dashes. Don't lead with "AI"; the intelligence is the how, not the pitch.
